How to buy

1. Pick it in the app

Cat menu → "Wardrobe". Tap a skin or accessory and confirm — a checkout page opens in your browser.

2. Pay

Card or SBP on Robokassa's secure page. Card details never pass through the app or through us.

3. Get your key

Right after payment you get a licence key and an "Open in Mewmori" button. The same key is emailed to you.

The key is cumulative: it carries all of your purchases at once and works on any number of your Macs. Lost it? Restore it by email. Outside Russia you can pay with Telegram Stars in the @mewmori_shop_bot bot.

More on payment methods and delivery times is on the payment and delivery page; the terms of purchase are in the public offer, and refunds are covered on the refunds page.
